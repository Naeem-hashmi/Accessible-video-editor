Keyboard reference

Keys without Control or Alt (letters, arrows, Space, Enter, Delete, brackets) work while the timeline or the layer list has focus. Each section is a heading and each table has column headers, so screen readers can move by heading (H) and by table cell.

File

File keys
KeysCommandWhat it does
Control NNew project from templateStart a new project from a template.
Control OOpen projectOpen a saved project.
Control SSave projectSave the project.
Control Shift SSave project asSave the project under a new name.
Control EExport videoMake the finished video or audio file.
Control Shift PProject settingsTitle, picture size and frame rate.
Control Shift commaPreferencesSpeech, sounds, step sizes and folders.

Edit

Edit keys
KeysCommandWhat it does
Control ZUndoUndo the last change. Says what was undone.
Control Y or Control Shift ZRedoRedo what you undid.
Control CCopy itemCopy the focused item.
Control XCut itemCopy the focused item and remove it.
Control VPaste itemPaste the copied item after the focused item.
Control DDuplicate itemPut a copy of the focused item right after it.
DeleteDelete itemDelete the focused item. With ripple on, later items move up to close the space.
Control DeleteDelete item, keep timingDelete the focused item and leave silence in its place, so later items do not move.
Shift DeleteDelete range on this layerRemove everything between range start and range end on the focused layer.
Control RRipple editing on or offWith ripple on, deleting, trimming and sliding move later items too. With ripple off, later items keep their time.
EnterItem optionsOpen all options for the focused item: length, volume, fades, transition, position, sound clean-up and equaliser.

Add

Add keys
KeysCommandWhat it does
Control IAdd files to end of layerChoose video, picture or audio files and add them after the last item of the focused layer. Sound files go to an audio layer automatically.
Control Shift IAdd files at playheadAdd files at the playhead time without moving anything; a free layer is used or created.
Control Shift EAdd sound effect or musicChoose a built-in or your own sound and add it at the playhead.
Control Shift CAdd colour cardAdd a solid colour picture, for a title background or a pause.
Control Shift VNew video layerAdd a video layer above the others.
Control Shift ANew audio layerAdd an audio layer.
Control Shift DeleteDelete layerDelete the focused layer and everything on it.
Alt EnterLayer optionsName, volume and defaults for new items on the focused layer.

Move around

Move around keys
KeysCommandWhat it does
left arrowPrevious itemGo to the previous item on this layer. The playhead moves to its start.
right arrowNext itemGo to the next item on this layer. The playhead moves to its start.
HomeFirst itemGo to the first item on this layer.
EndLast itemGo to the last item on this layer.
up arrowLayer above in the listGo to the previous layer, to the item at the playhead. Works with any number of layers.
down arrowLayer below in the listGo to the next layer, to the item at the playhead. Works with any number of layers.
Alt 1Go to layer 1Focus layer 1 and listen to it on its own.
Alt 2Go to layer 2Focus layer 2 and listen to it on its own.
Alt 3Go to layer 3Focus layer 3 and listen to it on its own.
Alt 4Go to layer 4Focus layer 4 and listen to it on its own.
Alt 5Go to layer 5Focus layer 5 and listen to it on its own.
Alt 6Go to layer 6Focus layer 6 and listen to it on its own.
Alt 7Go to layer 7Focus layer 7 and listen to it on its own.
Alt 8Go to layer 8Focus layer 8 and listen to it on its own.
Alt 9Go to layer 9Focus layer 9 and listen to it on its own.
Alt 0Listen to all layersPlay every layer together (muted layers stay silent). Press again to listen to the focused layer only.
Control LGo to layer numberType a layer number to jump to it. Useful with more than 9 layers.
Alt left arrowPrevious markerJump the playhead back to the previous marker, range start or range end.
Alt right arrowNext markerJump the playhead forward to the next marker, range start or range end.
Control GGo to timeType a time to move the playhead, for example 1:30, 90, or +5.
Control HomeGo to startMove the playhead to the start of the project.
Control EndGo to endMove the playhead to the end of the project.
F6Switch between timeline and layer listMove between the items of the focused layer and the list of all layers.

Arrange

Arrange keys
KeysCommandWhat it does
Alt Shift left arrowMove item earlier in orderSwap the focused item with the one before it.
Alt Shift right arrowMove item later in orderSwap the focused item with the one after it.
Alt Shift HomeMove item to the startMake the focused item the first on its layer.
Alt Shift EndMove item to the endMake the focused item the last on its layer.
Alt JSlide item earlierSlide the focused item earlier by the slide step (1 second unless changed in preferences).
Alt LSlide item laterSlide the focused item later by the slide step.
Alt Shift JSlide item earlier a littleSlide the focused item earlier by the fine step (a tenth of a second).
Alt Shift LSlide item later a littleSlide the focused item later by the fine step.
Control Shift JSlide whole layer earlierMove every item on the focused layer earlier by the slide step.
Control Shift LSlide whole layer laterMove every item on the focused layer later by the slide step.
Alt Shift up arrowMove layer up the listGive the focused layer a lower number. For video layers this puts its picture further back.
Alt Shift down arrowMove layer down the listGive the focused layer a higher number. For video layers this brings its picture to the front.

Cut and mark

Cut and mark keys
KeysCommandWhat it does
SSplit at playheadCut the item at the playhead into two items.
Shift left bracketTrim start to playheadRemove the part of the item before the playhead.
Shift right bracketTrim end to playheadRemove the part of the item after the playhead.
left bracketSet range startMark the playhead as the start of a range.
right bracketSet range endMark the playhead as the end of a range.
Control Shift RClear rangeRemove the range start and end marks.
BAdd markerPut a named marker at the playhead.
Shift BMarkers and their optionsList all markers to jump, rename, move, delete, split or add a transition there.
Control TTransition at playheadChoose the transition at the join nearest the playhead on the focused layer.

Play and listen

Play and listen keys
KeysCommandWhat it does
K or SpacePlay or pausePlay from the playhead, or pause.
LPlay forward, fasterPlay; press again for double and quadruple speed.
JJump backJump the playhead back by the jump step (5 seconds).
EscapeStopStop playing. The playhead stays where it stopped.
commaBack 1 secondMove the playhead back 1 second.
periodForward 1 secondMove the playhead forward 1 second.
Shift commaBack 10 secondsMove the playhead back 10 seconds.
Shift periodForward 10 secondsMove the playhead forward 10 seconds.
Control commaBack 1 frameMove the playhead back one frame.
Control periodForward 1 frameMove the playhead forward one frame.
MMute or unmute layerMute the focused layer. Muted layers are left out of playback and export.
Shift MSolo layerMute all other layers. Press again to unmute them.
Control Shift MUnmute all layersUnmute every layer.

Information

Information keys
KeysCommandWhat it does
TSay playhead timeSay where the playhead is.
Shift TSay project statusSay project length, layer, listening mode, ripple and range.
WWhat's at the playheadSay what every layer has at the playhead, and where pictures are placed.
DDescribe item in detailSay every setting of the focused item.
F7Check projectList possible problems: missing files, black gaps, muted layers and transitions that cannot play.
Shift VChange how much is spokenSwitch between brief, normal and detailed descriptions.
F1Keyboard helpList every command and its keys. Type to search.
