# Real recorded sounds

These recordings were made by people and shared on Wikimedia Commons in the public domain or under CC0. You may use them in any video, including commercial ones, with no credit needed. They were trimmed to at most 12 seconds, set to a peak of -1 dB and converted to MP3 for ClipScribe; the originals are at the links below.
