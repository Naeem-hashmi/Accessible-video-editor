Keyboard reference

Keys without Control or Alt (letters, arrows, Space, Enter, Delete, brackets) work while the timeline, the layer list or the preview has focus. Each section is a heading and each table has column headers, so screen readers can move by heading (H) and by table cell.

Laptops: every command has at least one key that needs no number pad, no Fn key (Home, End, F1 to F12) and no AltGr (brackets on many non-US keyboards). Where a command lists two keys, the second is the laptop-friendly one: for example I and O for range start and end, Control Left and Control Right for first and last item, Control H for help and Alt period for the next area.

Keys inside position mode

Press P on a picture, video or text to enter position mode. A short tone plays after each move: it is panned left or right by where the picture is across the frame, and higher the nearer it is to the top.

Position mode keys
KeysWhat it does
ArrowsMove 5 percent of the frame. With Shift, 1 percent.
Plus and minusBigger and smaller. With Shift, in small steps.
1 to 9 (the number row above the letters, or a number pad)Snap to a place laid out like a phone keypad upside down, or a number pad: 7 top left, 8 top centre, 9 top right, 4 middle left, 5 centre, 6 middle right, 1 bottom left, 2 bottom centre, 3 bottom right. No number pad is needed.
0 (number row or number pad)Full frame (maximise).
FFull frame only: switch between fit (whole picture, maybe with bars) and fill (covers the frame, edges cut off).

O and Shift OMore and less opacity, in steps of 10 percent.
